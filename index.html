A little about Dom

At ease in
every kitchen.

Dom Wise brings a steady hand, a curious palate, and a generous sense of hospitality to every kitchen he enters. He is a versatile chef who feels at home across different environments, adapting his approach to the people, pace, and purpose of the meal. For Dom, good cooking starts with paying attention: who is coming to the table, what they enjoy, and how food can make a moment feel more welcoming.

He currently works at Marzae Cellar + Provisions in Boston’s Haymarket area, continuing to grow his craft in a professional kitchen. Outside that setting, Dom brings thoughtful cooking into private homes through intimate dinners, weekly meal preparation, and family chef support. A dinner at home can be planned around the occasion and the guests; meal preparation can reflect a household’s preferences and the week ahead; family support can be shaped around agreed schedules and responsibilities.

Working with Dom begins with a conversation rather than a preset menu. He takes time to understand tastes, dietary needs, routines, and the kind of support a household is looking for, then helps make a clear plan. His aim is to make the food feel considered and the experience feel easy—whether that means giving a host more time with guests, bringing order to a busy week, or helping a family feel well looked after.
